# Annotations Directory Index (`data/annotations/`)

This directory contains human-evaluation packages, evaluation guidelines, blank rater templates, model-drafted evaluation sheets, and completed rating workbooks across the project's evaluation phases.

---

## Artifact Taxonomy

To prevent confusion between similarly named files, the artifacts are categorized into four distinct functional classes:

### 1. Completed Evaluations & Spot-Checks (Authoritative Data)
These files contain actual human evaluation scores used in reports and analyses:
- **`corrected_rerun_human_eval_rater1_filled.csv`** — **Primary authoritative evaluation for the pre-registered corrected rerun.** Contains 72 questions × 3 systems (V, A, C) evaluated across Correctness, Completeness, Groundedness, Relevance (1–5 scale), and Unsupported Claims (binary).
  - *Provenance:* Review of the AI-drafted template by rater 1 (99.2% of scores accepted unchanged; effectively an AI judge verified by a single human).
- **`corrected_rerun_spotcheck_rater_IS.xlsx`** — Original evaluation workbook completed by independent human rater **IS** for 20 randomly sampled questions, completely blinded to prior ratings.
- **`corrected_rerun_spotcheck_filled.csv`** — Normalized CSV imported from `corrected_rerun_spotcheck_rater_IS.xlsx` via `src.evaluation.import_spotcheck_workbook`. Used by `src.evaluation.analyze_corrected_rerun_spotcheck`.
- **`corrected_rerun_spotcheck2_sheet_export.csv` / `corrected_rerun_spotcheck2_filled.csv`** — Completed ratings from an exploratory second independent checker, added post hoc (§17 of pre-registration).
- **`relation_aware_human_eval_filled.csv`** — Completed human evaluation for the earlier relation-aware experiment (produced under context truncation).
- **`generation_fix_heldout_human_eval_filled.csv`** — Completed evaluation for the 13 held-out items in the two-stage generation experiment.

---

### 2. Audit-Only Model Drafts
- **`corrected_rerun_rater1_model_draft_AUDIT_ONLY.csv`** — The raw LLM-generated rating draft produced before human review.
  - *Purpose:* Retained strictly for audit transparency to demonstrate exactly what was model-drafted versus what was human-modified during rater 1's review.

---

### 3. Blank Rater Templates (Distributed to Evaluators)
Unfilled sheets generated by package builder scripts (`src/evaluation/build_*_human_package.py`) with blinded system identifiers:
- **`corrected_rerun_human_eval_rater1.csv` & `corrected_rerun_human_eval_rater2.csv`** — Unfilled templates for the corrected rerun.
  - *Note on Duplication:* These two files are byte-identical because the builder script generates individualized file paths for separate raters to maintain independent scoring streams without cross-rater contamination.
- **`corrected_rerun_spotcheck.csv`** — Blank 20-question spotcheck template provided to independent evaluator IS.
- **`final_structured_kg_human_eval.csv` & `final_structured_kg_human_eval_v2.csv`** — Blank templates for Phase 7 structured KG evaluation.
- **`generation_fix_human_eval.csv` & `generation_fix_heldout_human_eval.csv`** — Blank templates for the two-stage generation experiment.
- **`relation_aware_human_eval.csv`** — Blank template for the initial 7-system relation-aware study.
- **`human_eval_template.csv` & `retrieval_eval_template.json`** — General evaluation templates from pilot phases.
- **`canonical_180_kg_relevance.jsonl`** — Blank audit template for assessing whether the 180 canonical questions strictly required KG paths.

---

### 4. Human-Evaluation Guidelines
Standardized rating rubrics and scoring instructions provided to evaluators:
- `corrected_rerun_human_eval_guidelines.md`: 5-point Likert criteria for Correctness, Completeness, Groundedness, Relevance, and binary Unsupported Claims in the corrected rerun.
- `canonical_180_kg_relevance_guidelines.md`: Protocol for classifying questions as KG-required, KG-helpful, or Direct-fact.
- `final_structured_kg_human_eval_guidelines.md` & `final_structured_kg_human_eval_v2_guidelines.md`: Rubric for Phase 7 structured KG evaluation.
- `generation_fix_human_eval_guidelines.md` & `generation_fix_heldout_human_eval_guidelines.md`: Rubric for two-stage evidence plan evaluation.
- `relation_aware_human_eval_guidelines.md`: Protocol for evaluating relation-aware retrieval vs dense baselines.
- `relational_qa_guidelines.md`: Guidelines for the initial 62-question relational QA benchmark.
- `human_eval_guidelines.md`: General rating guidelines from pilot studies.
